mblint

Privacy

This page covers mblint's simulator at mblint.shortarrow.jp, its marketplace at marketplace.mblint.shortarrow.jp and its Storybook at storybook.mblint.shortarrow.jp. None of them asks you to sign in or sets a cookie of its own.

Cloudflare Web Analytics

The sites are served by Cloudflare, which adds the script of Cloudflare Web Analytics to their pages. For each visit, the script reports the page's address, the site that linked to it, your country, browser, operating system and type of device, and how long the page took to load. The list of what it records is Cloudflare's.

Cloudflare states that Web Analytics uses no state kept in your browser, such as a cookie or local storage, to collect these figures, and that Cloudflare does not fingerprint visitors by their IP address, their browser's User-Agent or any other data for the purpose of displaying analytics. See Cloudflare Web Analytics.

What the simulator keeps in your browser

The simulator keeps how it is displayed in your browser's local storage: the theme, whether the names of the controls are shown, which sidebars and lists are open, and how wide and tall they are. It sends none of this anywhere.

Files you load or edit stay in the browser tab and are gone when it closes, and a rig you export is saved only to your own computer. The simulator sends none of them anywhere.

Other sites your browser asks

  • The simulator and the marketplace load their typefaces from Google Fonts, so your browser asks fonts.googleapis.com and fonts.gstatic.com for them.
  • When you load a repository into the simulator, or open a link that names one, your browser asks api.github.com and raw.githubusercontent.com for its files, without signing in.

Each of these sites receives your IP address and your browser's request, and handles them under its own policy.
